The story of Chico Soul Cafe is a testament to friendship, passion, and a shared desire to spread the richness of Mexican culture through a cup of coffee. It was conceived as a place where Mexican flavors could flourish and represent the identity of a nation steeped in history and tradition.

In our shop, we believe in community. Come and see our interactive photo op spaces while you wait for your curated drinks.
